Data & Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short

  • We only use your information to reply to you and to provide the services you ask for.
  • We never sell it, and we share it only with the services that help us run the website and the masjid, or where the law requires.
  • Analytics cookies are off unless you allow them. Maps and videos load only when you ask for them.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time: info@greenwichmadinatrust.org.uk.

Who we are

Greenwich Madina Trust (“we”, “us”) runs Ghousia Masjid and its Educational Centre. We are a registered charity in England and Wales (No. 1129277) and the controller of the personal information described here.

Write to us at 56 Camrose Street, London SE2 0JA, or email info@greenwichmadinatrust.org.uk.

What we collect, and why

When you send us a message

The contact form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number, a subject and your message. The form is run by Formspree, which passes your message to our email inbox and keeps a copy for us. We use what you send only to reply and to help with your request. Emails and calls to us are handled in the same way. Our email is provided by Google Workspace.

When you use our services

To arrange a Janazah, a Nikah, counselling or Madrassah enrolment, we need details about you and, sometimes, members of your family. We use them only to provide that service. Because we are a masjid, this information can show your religious belief, so we treat it with extra care: it stays within the Trust and is shared outside it only as described below or with your agreement.

  • Nikah certificate: the form on our website stays on your device. Nothing you type is sent to us or stored online; you print it or save it, and bring or email it to us.
  • Madrassah enrolment: parents complete the paper form. We use the details only to run classes and keep children safe.
  • Janazah: with the family’s agreement, we share what is needed with funeral directors, cemeteries and the registrar.
  • Counselling: conversations are confidential. We would share information only if someone were at risk of serious harm, or if the law required it.

When you donate

Online donations are made on Hibabox’s secure website, not ours. Hibabox processes your payment and, if you choose it, your Gift Aid declaration, under its own privacy policy. We keep the donation and Gift Aid records we need, for as long as HMRC requires.

When you visit the website

Like every website, ours receives your device’s IP address and basic browser details, because these are needed to send you the pages. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which records this in its security logs. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics also counts your visit (see Cookies).

Our legal reasons

  • Replying to you and providing services you ask for: our legitimate interest in serving our community, or steps you ask us to take.
  • Information that shows religious belief: the condition for not-for-profit religious bodies dealing with people in regular contact with them, or your explicit consent where we ask for it.
  • Donation, Gift Aid and charity records: our legal obligations.
  • Analytics cookies: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We use these services to run the website and the masjid:

  • Formspree: the contact form.
  • Google: email (Google Workspace), and Google Analytics only if you allow it.
  • GitHub: hosts the website.
  • Hibabox: online donations.

We also share information with funeral directors and similar organisations when you ask us to, and with the authorities when the law requires it. Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States; they protect your information with safeguards recognised in UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge or standard contract clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Messages and emails: until your enquiry is resolved, and then for up to two years in case you contact us again about it.
  • Service records (Nikah, Janazah, Madrassah): for as long as they are needed for that service, and for any longer period the law or our safeguarding duties require.
  • Donation and Gift Aid records: six years, as HMRC requires.
  • Analytics data: Google keeps the detailed records for no more than 14 months; after that only anonymous totals remain.

Cookies and similar technologies

A bar at the foot of the page asks whether we may use analytics cookies. Nothing optional is set until you choose “Allow analytics”, and you can change your mind at any time with , also at the foot of every page.

Maps, videos and our Instagram feed

  • Maps: Google Maps loads only when you press “Show map”. Google may then set its own cookies.
  • Videos: videos play from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode, and only when you press play.
  • Instagram feed: the homepage shows our latest Instagram posts through a service called Behold, which says it sets no cookies.
  • Fonts and libraries: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and programming libraries from jsDelivr. They receive your IP address but set no cookies.

Links to Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

Our website is not designed to collect information from children. Information about children in our Madrassah comes from their parents or guardians, who can ask to see, correct or remove it at any time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, or limit how we use it;
  • stop using it, if we rely on our legitimate interests;
  • give it to you, or to another organisation, in a common format.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email info@greenwichmadinatrust.org.uk; we will reply within one month.

Complaints

Please tell us first if you are unhappy with how we have handled your information. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
